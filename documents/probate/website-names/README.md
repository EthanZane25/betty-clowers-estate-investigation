# Website Filing Names

These filenames match the filing labels shown on the Georgia Probate Records estate docket for Betty Jean Clowers, Case 26PV49392.

Files included here are copies of PDFs already obtained and reviewed. Their PDF contents are unchanged; only the filenames were changed for navigation.

## Filing names shown on the website

- PETITION TO PROBATE WILL IN SOLEMN FORM
- WILL
- ORDER FOR SERVICE OF NOTICE
- NOTICE
- CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE
- LETTER
- RETURN OF SHERIFF
- OATH
- ORDER ADMITTING WILL
- LETTERS TESTAMENTARY - NOT RELIEVED
- Petition to Probate Will in Solemn Form
- Declination to Serve as Personal Representative
- Notice of Claim or Claim of Creditor

## Important gaps

**RETURN OF SHERIFF** is listed on the website, but a valid PDF copy was not among the supplied files when this package was built. It is therefore not represented by a fabricated or placeholder PDF.

**Declination to Serve as Personal Representative** is listed separately on the docket. It may be contained within a previously saved multi-page petition packet, but it has not been separated into its own independently verified PDF in this package.

`ESTATE DETAILS.pdf` is the saved estate docket/details page; it is not itself one of the filing labels.
