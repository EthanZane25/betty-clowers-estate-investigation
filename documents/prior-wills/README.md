**STATUS: SOURCE PHOTOGRAPHS PRESERVED / UNCERTIFIED COPY**

On August 30, 2026, four JPEG photographs supplied to the investigation were identified as the four pages of Betty Jean Clowers's April 1, 2008 Last Will and Testament. The repository preserves those photographs unchanged as the source-level files.

This four-page PDF was assembled from the four JPEG source photographs for easier reading. It is a **derived convenience file**, not a certified copy, not an original court download, and not a substitute for the source JPEGs. No textual reconstruction was used.

### Source-verified provisions visible in the photographs

The photographed copy shows that the 2008 will:
- identifies Betty as a resident of Bibb County, Georgia;
- contains burial/funeral instructions in Item 1;
- appoints Andre Washington and Dave Dennis III as executors, with Tiffany Clowers Hall as successor executrix;
- gives Donald Wright a life estate in 1661 Lawton Avenue;
- at termination of the life estate, divides Betty's interest among Keyvean Sheheed Clowers, Tiffany Clowers Hall, Andre Washington, Dave Dennis III, Starla Wright, and Bertha Anderson and directs sale/equal division of proceeds after outstanding bills/debt;
- gives household goods/furniture to the Macon Rescue Mission;
- gives bank-account funds to Andre Washington and Dave Dennis III equally;
- addresses life-insurance policies on which Andre Washington and Dave Dennis III are listed as beneficiaries;
- is signed/attested and dated April 1, 2008; and
- does not name Frances Brooks Thornton in the four-page copy.

### Integrity / provenance limitation

The photographs establish what appears on the supplied copy, but the repository does not currently establish the complete chain of custody of the paper document photographed. The copy is not represented as certified. Investigation should still seek:

4. witness/notary verification and source provenance.
