# Financial and Asset Tracing Plan

## Objective

Determine the full asset picture before death, identify changes during Betty's final year, distinguish probate from non-probate transfers, and trace estate administration after death.

This is an investigative checklist. Private financial records should be obtained only through lawful authority, an authorized estate representative, consent, subpoena, or discovery.

## Review Window

Primary review: **August 2024 through October 24, 2025**.

Extend earlier where needed to establish baseline and later to trace estate administration.

Use an enhanced review window around **August 4, 2025**, the will-execution date.

## Bank Accounts

For each institution:
- account ownership history;
- signature cards;
- joint-owner additions/removals;
- authorized signers;
- POD beneficiary history;
- statements;
- canceled checks;
- deposits;
- cash withdrawals;
- debit-card transactions;
- electronic transfers;
- safe-deposit access;
- new accounts;
- account closures;
- POA documents used;
- fraud/identity-verification records.

Flag transactions involving:
- Frances;
- Frances-related accounts/entities;
- drafting lawyer;
- witnesses;
- funeral home;
- unexplained cash withdrawals;
- new joint ownership.

## Retirement / Investments / Annuities

Trace:
- account balances;
- beneficiary-designation history;
- changes and dates;
- distributions;
- withdrawals;
- rollovers;
- account owner/authorized-person changes;
- correspondence;
- recorded calls if retained.

## Life Insurance

For each carrier:
- original policy;
- owner;
- insured;
- beneficiary history;
- change forms;
- policy loans;
- withdrawals;
- surrender/cash-out;
- payment destination;
- identity verification;
- POA use;
- recorded calls;
- correspondence.

## Personal Property

Identify:

- safe-deposit contents;
- business interests;
- collectibles;
- claims/refunds;
- final pension/paycheck/benefits.

## Credit Report

A deceased person's credit report is not a public record. If an authorized estate representative or counsel can lawfully obtain it, it may identify:
- open credit;
- recent inquiries;
- newly opened accounts;
- outstanding loans;
- creditor addresses.

Do not publish sensitive credit-report data in this public repository.

## Medicare / Medicaid / Facility Billing

If Betty had Medicare/Medicaid, authorized records may help reconstruct:
- providers;
- dates of service;
- facilities;
- billing;
- responsible-party information;
- care timeline.

A fraud complaint should not be filed merely because billing exists. Use official reporting channels only if specific evidence supports suspected fraud or abuse.

## Transaction Timeline

For every material transaction, log:
- date;
- account/asset;
- amount/value;
- sender/grantor;
- recipient/grantee;
- method;
- document authorizer;
- reason stated;
- source record;
- whether Frances participated;
- relation to August 4 will;
- evidence status.

## Non-Probate vs. Probate

Do not assume all assets pass under the will. Identify assets that may pass outside probate:
- joint tenancy/survivorship;
- POD/TOD;
- life-insurance beneficiary;
- retirement beneficiary;
- trusts;
- transfer-on-death mechanisms where applicable.

## Estate Administration After Death

Trace:
- estate EIN;
- estate account opening;
- deposits;
- creditor payments;
- property expenses;
- attorney fees;
- executor compensation;
- distributions;
- sale proceeds;
- refunds;
- tax payments.

Reconcile the financial records to the estate inventory and annual returns.
