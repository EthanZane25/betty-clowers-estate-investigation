# Public Records and Research Sources

Use official sources first. Preserve screenshots/PDFs, search criteria, date accessed, and source URLs.

## Bibb County Probate / Georgia Probate Records

Estate case:

https://www.georgiaprobaterecords.com/Estates/EstateDetails.aspx?RECID=ODkxMTA1

Online copies are not certified. Certified copies should be obtained from Bibb County Probate Court.

## Georgia Open Records Act

For Georgia state/local agency records, use the **Georgia Open Records Act**, not a generic federal FOIA request.

Georgia Attorney General guidance:
https://law.georgia.gov/key-issues/open-government/how-make-open-records-request

The Attorney General states that requests should be directed to the agency's records custodian and that written requests are preferable for documentation/enforcement.

## Georgia Notary Index

Georgia Superior Court Clerks' Cooperative Authority (GSCCCA) Notary Index:
https://www.gsccca.org/learn/search-systems/notary-index

The GSCCCA states that the index is free and can be searched by name, city, and/or ZIP.

## Georgia Professional Licensing

Georgia Secretary of State — GOALS:
https://sos.ga.gov/goals

Public license verification:
https://verify.sos.ga.gov/online/

Use this to test whether an identity has a regulated professional license and to review available public board orders. Do not assume a paid caregiver was required to hold a nursing license; first establish the services performed and applicable credential.

## South Carolina Professional Licensing

South Carolina Department of Labor, Licensing and Regulation:
https://www.llr.sc.gov/

Use the appropriate board's Licensee Lookup / License Verification and public board orders.

## State Bar of Georgia

Member Directory:
https://prod.gabar.org/member-directory

The directory provides public membership and disciplinary-history information for Georgia attorneys.

## Federal Bankruptcy / Federal Court

PACER:
https://pacer.uscourts.gov/

Use verified identity anchors before attributing any bankruptcy or federal case to a person with a similar name.

## Property / Deeds / Liens

Use the Bibb County/Georgia real-estate recording system and GSCCCA sources for:
- deeds;
- security deeds;
- releases;
- liens;
- plats;
- recorded judgments where available.

Record instrument number/book/page and download the actual instrument rather than relying only on commercial property aggregators.

## Civil Litigation

Search the appropriate Georgia and South Carolina court systems using exact identity anchors. Commercial aggregators can be leads, not substitutes for the filed docket/document.

## Public Social Media

Public social-media material may be used only for legitimate timeline/relationship research.

Preserve:
- URL;
- date accessed;
- screenshot;
- context.

Do not:
- impersonate people;
- obtain private-account content through deception;
- harass witnesses;
- publish irrelevant private details.

## Private Records Are Different

Banking, credit, medical, phone, email, text, and private account records are not ordinary public-record sources. Obtain them only through lawful authority, consent, an authorized estate representative, subpoena, or discovery as appropriate.
