# Public Research Rules

This repository is public.

Do not publish:
- Social Security numbers
- full bank-account numbers
- full insurance policy numbers
- private medical charts
- private phone numbers not already legitimately public
- passwords or login credentials
- identity documents
- protected health information unless legally appropriate to publish

Researchers should:
- cite sources
- distinguish facts from allegations
- preserve original URLs/documents
- avoid doxxing
- avoid harassment
- avoid contacting witnesses deceptively
- avoid asserting criminal conduct without evidence

Useful submissions include:
- deed references
- docket entries
- probate filings
- archived public records
- court decisions
- professional-license records
- cemetery records
- obituary records
- public business/caregiver records
