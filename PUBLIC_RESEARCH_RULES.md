# Public Research Rules

This repository is public.

## Do Not Publish

- Social Security numbers;
- full bank-account numbers;
- full insurance policy numbers;
- private medical charts or protected health information unless legally appropriate to publish;
- private phone numbers or email addresses that are not necessary to the public record;
- passwords/login credentials;
- identity documents;
- private credit reports;
- private text/email/phone records obtained without lawful authority;
- unnecessary personal information unrelated to the investigation.

## Evidence Discipline

Researchers should:
- cite sources;
- preserve original URLs/documents;
- distinguish facts from allegations;
- distinguish certified copies from web copies;
- use evidence-status labels;
- preserve uncertainty in handwriting/name transcription;
- avoid attributing same-name records without identity corroboration;
- avoid asserting criminal conduct without evidence.

## Lawful Access

Do not:
- bypass account security;
- impersonate a witness;
- use deception to obtain private records;
- access financial, medical, email, phone, or social-media accounts without lawful authority.

Private records should be sought through consent, authorized estate authority, counsel, subpoena, discovery, or other lawful process.

## Witness / Person Research

Public-record research may include:
- professional licenses;
- public disciplinary orders;
- recorded property interests;
- public court cases;
- bankruptcy dockets;
- recorded liens/judgments;
- public social-media material relevant to timeline/relationships.

Do not dox or harass people.

## Source Preservation

For every important record:
- preserve the original file;
- record source/custodian;
- record date obtained;
- save source URL;
- compute SHA-256;
- avoid modifying the source copy;
- keep working annotations separate.

## Useful Submissions

- deed references and actual instruments;
- probate filings;
- archived public records;
- court decisions;
- professional-license records;
- cemetery/funeral public records;
- obituary records;
- public business/caregiver records;
- source copies of prior wills;
- records establishing dates and relationships.
