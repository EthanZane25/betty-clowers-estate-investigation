# Betty Jean Clowers Estate Investigation

## Purpose

This repository is a public evidence and research file concerning the estate of **Betty Jean Clowers**, Bibb County Probate Court case **26PV49392**.

It is intended to organize public records, preserve source documents, identify documentary contradictions, and track questions requiring further verification. It does **not** presume criminal wrongdoing by any person.

## Evidence Rules

Use these labels consistently:

- **DOCUMENTED** — supported by a primary record.
- **CORROBORATED** — supported by multiple independent sources.
- **REPORTED** — supplied by a witness/family member but not independently proven.
- **LEAD** — requires further verification.
- **DISPUTED** — conflicting evidence exists.
- **INFERENCE** — interpretation, not itself a proven fact.
- **NOT ESTABLISHED** — evidence is insufficient.

## Central Documented Sequence

### Earlier estate plan — April 1, 2008

Source photographs of a four-page April 1, 2008 will have now been obtained and preserved in `documents/prior-wills/source-images/`. A convenience PDF assembled from those photographs is in `documents/prior-wills/original/`. The source photographs show that the 2008 will:

- identified Betty as a Bibb County, Georgia resident;
- gave Donald Wright a life estate in 1661 Lawton Avenue;
- included remainder interests for Keyvean Sheheed Clowers, Tiffany Clowers Hall, Andre Washington, Dave Dennis III, Starla Wright, and Bertha Anderson;
- named Andre Washington and Dave Dennis III as executors, with Tiffany Clowers Hall as successor executor;
- contained burial instructions; and
- did not make Frances Brooks Thornton a principal beneficiary.

**Source-status:** the repository now preserves the four source photographs supplied to the investigation. They are not represented as a certified court copy or the original paper will. The assembled PDF is a convenience derivative and the JPEG source files remain the evidentiary source copies. See `documents/prior-wills/README.md`.

### Later estate plan — August 4, 2025

**DOCUMENTED:** The probate-filed August 4, 2025 will:

- states that Betty was a resident of and domiciled in **Aiken County, South Carolina**;
- describes **Frances Brooks Thornton as Betty's "daughter and caretaker"**;
- gives Frances Betty's interest in **1661 Lawton Avenue** if Frances survives Betty;
- directs Frances to sell the Lawton Avenue home;
- names **Dave Dennis III** as first personal representative; and
- names Frances as successor personal representative if Dave Dennis III cannot serve.

The will was executed approximately 81 days before Betty's death.

### Probate petition — March 5, 2026

**DOCUMENTED:** Frances's Petition to Probate Will in Solemn Form materially conflicts with the later will in several respects:

- the petition identifies Betty's place of domicile as **1661 Lawton Avenue, Macon, Bibb County, Georgia 31204**;
- it identifies **Kayveon Clowers** as Betty's surviving biological child and son;
- it expressly states that Frances was named in the will as Betty's daughter but **"is not a biological or adopted daughter"**; and
- it identifies Frances as Betty's **goddaughter**.

The petition also requests publication because Kayveon's address was described as unknown, even though the petition's first page lists a Macon address for Kayveon. This internal inconsistency requires procedural review and should be compared with the Order for Service, Notice, Sheriff's Return, and any deputy records.

### Dave Dennis III declines; Frances becomes executor

**DOCUMENTED:** A renunciation signed by Dave Dennis III on February 6, 2026 states that he declines to serve and consents to Frances Brooks Thornton, the named successor personal representative, serving as sole personal representative.

**DOCUMENTED:** The probate docket later lists Frances as **EXECUTOR**, and Letters Testamentary — **NOT RELIEVED** — were issued April 22, 2026.

## Material Change Involving Betty's Caregiver

A central issue is the substantial change between the earlier 2008 estate plan and the August 4, 2025 will.

The currently assembled evidence supports this statement:

> During the period in which Frances Brooks Thornton was described in the later will as Betty's caretaker, Betty's estate plan changed materially in a manner that gave Frances a substantial beneficial interest in Betty's Lawton Avenue property and placed Frances in position to administer the estate. After the initially nominated personal representative declined, Frances became executor/personal representative.

The evidence presently collected **does not by itself establish** that Frances personally caused, directed, procured, drafted, pressured Betty into, or otherwise improperly influenced the 2025 will. Determining Frances's actual role in arranging the lawyer, transporting Betty, communicating with the drafting lawyer, supplying information, paying fees, reviewing drafts, selecting witnesses/notary, retaining the original will, or participating in execution is a central investigative issue.

## Major Documentary Conflicts

1. **Relationship:** 2025 will — "daughter and caretaker"; 2026 petition — not biological or adopted daughter, goddaughter; public docket — relationship field "DAUGHTER."
2. **Domicile:** 2025 will — Aiken County, South Carolina; 2026 petition — 1661 Lawton Avenue, Macon, Bibb County, Georgia.
3. **Heir/service address:** petition identifies Kayveon as the sole surviving biological child and lists a Macon address, while another portion requests publication because his address was said to be unknown.
4. **Service:** family reports Kayveon was not personally served; the official Return of Sheriff is now preserved in the repository and requires line-by-line review.
5. **Prior estate plan:** four source photographs of the 2008 will are now preserved; certification/provenance beyond the supplied copy remains to be developed.
