# Estate Administration Checklist

## Why This Track Matters

The public docket lists **Letters Testamentary — NOT RELIEVED** issued April 22, 2026.

Georgia reporting duties can be affected by the will, waivers, consents, and court orders. Obtain the actual filings before concluding exactly what Frances was required to file.

## Records

- inventory;
- supplemental inventory;
- annual returns;
- intermediate reports;
- estate EIN;
- estate bank account;
- opening balance;
- appraisals;
- property valuations;
- creditor notices/claims;
- payments to PruittHealth and other creditors;
- funeral expenses;
- property expenses;
- taxes;
- insurance;
- attorney fees;
- executor compensation;
- distributions;
- receipts/releases from beneficiaries;
- petitions/orders concerning sale;
- deeds;
- closing statements;
- final return;
- settlement/discharge.

## Reconciliation

Build a ledger:

| Date | Asset / Transaction | Amount | Source | Destination | Authority | Supporting Record | Status |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|

Reconcile:
1. date-of-death assets;
2. inventory;
3. later-discovered assets;
4. estate deposits;
5. expenses/creditors;
6. property sale proceeds;
7. distributions;
8. closing balance.

## Key Questions

- Was every known probate asset inventoried?
- Were non-probate assets correctly distinguished?
- Were estate funds kept separate?
- Were creditor claims handled properly?
- Were property sale proceeds deposited to the estate when required?
- Were executor/attorney fees documented?
- Were beneficiaries/heirs entitled to receive inventory/returns, and were any rights validly waived?
- Has any discharge been sought?
