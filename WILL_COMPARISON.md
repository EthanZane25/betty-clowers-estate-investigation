## What the comparison establishes

The preserved 2008 source photographs and the 2025 probate copy establish that the two estate plans are materially different. The 2025 plan gives Frances a substantially enhanced beneficial and fiduciary position: she receives Betty's interest in the primary property, is directed to sell it, and is named successor personal representative.

After Dave Dennis III declined in February 2026, Frances in fact became the estate's executor/personal representative.

## What the comparison does not establish

The change in disposition does **not by itself prove** that Frances caused, procured, directed, drafted, coerced, or unduly influenced the 2025 will.

That question turns on evidence such as:
- who contacted and selected the drafting lawyer;
- who scheduled the appointment;
- who transported Betty;
- who supplied family/asset information;
- who paid the legal bill;
- who received or reviewed drafts;
- whether Betty met privately with counsel;
- who selected the witnesses/notary;
- whether Frances was present before/during/after execution;
- who retained the original will;
- contemporaneous medical/capacity evidence;
- financial and beneficiary changes during the same period.
