## For Every Recorded Instrument

Record:
- instrument number/book/page;
- execution date;
- recording date;
- grantor;
- grantee;
- consideration;
- preparer;
- witnesses;
- notary;
- return-to address;
- legal description;
- prior-deed reference;
- estate capacity in which Frances signed;
- proceeds destination if available.

## Evidence Rule

Do not infer that a deed transfer occurred merely because the will authorizes or directs a sale. Obtain the actual recorded instrument and trace consideration/proceeds.
